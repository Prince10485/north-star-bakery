How we began

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: make dependable, delicious baked goods and create a friendly place for the neighborhood to gather. We prepare familiar favorites alongside seasonal specials and aim to make every visit welcoming.

Our sourcing philosophy

We look for quality ingredients and work with suppliers when practical to support freshness and responsible sourcing. Ingredients and availability may vary by recipe, so customers with allergies should contact us before ordering.

The people behind the counter

Our bakers, counter team, and order coordinators work together to prepare daily favorites and help customers plan special occasions. When you visit, tell us what you are celebrating—we enjoy being part of the moment.

Come say hello
